MY PROCESS

My Development Workflow

I follow a structured development workflow to turn ideas into modern, responsive, and user-friendly web experiences.

01

Plan & Brainstorm

I define the project's goals, features, and layout, then use AI to brainstorm ideas and explore possible solutions.

02

AI-Assisted Development

I use AI tools to accelerate research, brainstorming, coding assistance, debugging, and problem solving. I review, test, and refine the implementation before it becomes part of the final project.

03

Build in VS Code

I develop the project step by step in Visual Studio Code, reviewing, understanding, and refining each part to ensure clean, reliable, and maintainable code.

04

Organize the Project

I maintain a clean, scalable project structure, by organizing HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and assets for easier maintenance and future updates.

05

Test & Improve

I thoroughly test the website, fix issues, improve responsiveness, optimize performance, and refine the overall user experience.

06

Final Review

Before deployment, I perform a final review, clean up the code, optimize the project, and make final improvements to deliver a polished, and professional website.

This workflow helps me transform ideas into modern, responsive, and user-friendly websites that are functional, efficient, and impactful. 🚀
